2. Information We Collect *

We collect and process the information described below to run the service, protect accounts, complete order workflows, meet legal obligations, and support users.

  • Account and authentication Email address, Firebase user ID, display name, profile photo URL, phone number, email verification state, anonymous state, and sign-in provider IDs. Firebase Authentication handles passwords and authentication tokens; ClearFix does not store your password in Firestore.
  • Profile and preferences Display name, phone number, preferred language, profile photo, and saved address details such as country, province, city, postal code, street lines, unit or suite, buzzer, floor, PO Box, rural route, site, compartment, box, lot, concession, legal land description, and access notes when you choose to provide them.
  • Orders and service requests Order title, description, category, status, status history, photos, address and access details, optional location coordinates used for address suggestions, schedule, urgency, budget in CAD, tax receipt preference, contact name, contact phone, preferred contact channel, customer notes, closure reasons, and timestamps.
  • Specialist proposals and accepted terms Provider and customer IDs, provider display or company name, provider contact details needed for the order, proposed price, schedule, terms, warranty notes, proposal state, accepted provider snapshot, and related timestamps.
  • Messages and conversations Conversation participants, order or proposal context, message text, message sender, participant role, client message ID, sent time, read state, and last-message previews used to show conversation lists and unread badges.
  • Photos, files, and metadata Profile photos and order photos selected from your camera, gallery, or file picker, plus metadata such as original file name, content type, size, image dimensions, source, and upload time.
  • Location and address autofill If you grant device location permission, the app may request your current coordinates and send them through a Firebase Cloud Function to Google Geocoding so it can suggest a Canadian address. When a map is displayed, the app requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap Foundation infrastructure. Those requests can include the requested tile coordinates, which indicate the approximate map area being viewed, and technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and referrer when supplied by the device or browser. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings.
  • Technical and support data Device platform, browser or operating system details, app and build version, locale, time zone, route and lifecycle context, error type, message and stack trace, session and limited performance context, and support messages you send to us. Firebase and other infrastructure providers may also process technical request metadata such as an IP address. We use this information to secure the service, diagnose failures, measure crash-free sessions, and respond to requests. The ClearFix Sentry SDK disables default PII, screenshots, view hierarchy, SDK logs, and automatic interaction data, and removes the event user object before sending an event. Sentry and its network infrastructure may still receive technical request data, including an IP address, subject to the project settings and Sentry controls described below.
  • Consent and moderation records Accepted legal-document versions and acceptance times; complaints; moderation warnings and their acknowledgement times; reason codes and moderator messages; temporary-block start and end times; permanent-ban state; and audit or evidence records reasonably needed to apply, review, or defend a moderation decision.
  • Commission payment records Commission amount and currency, payment status, Checkout Session and event identifiers, attempt and return status, timestamps, receipt reference, and reconciliation records. Payment-method details entered on Stripe Checkout are collected and processed by Stripe, not stored by ClearFix in Firestore.

In this policy, public professional information means information available within the authenticated ClearFix service; it is not made available through an unauthenticated public profile. History may include an order’s title, outcome, dates, closure reasons and comments, complaint indicators, and order unavailability. A public profile does not itself disclose email, phone number, or home address. Contact and site access details are disclosed to order participants according to their role and the order stage.

In connection with an order, we process proposals, agreed terms, the parties’ responses, work dates and outcomes, closure reasons and comments, messages, and complaints. While a complaint dialogue is active, any active signed-in user who can access the order can read it. Only the customer and appointed provider can post complaint messages; other viewers have read-only access. After the complaint is resolved, its messages are hidden from ordinary users but may be retained for moderation, disputes, security, and legal records. Ordinary private correspondence does not become public. A complaint or counter is not a finding of fault, dishonesty, or lack of qualification.

Posting, receiving, storing, technically processing, or displaying a profile, document, message, or complaint does not by itself mean that ClearFix has verified the information, confirmed a specialist’s qualifications or honesty, established a violation, or endorsed a user’s conduct. ClearFix does not currently verify each specialist or professional document before display and does not continuously supervise services. A profile, order history, platform indicators, and absence of account restrictions are not a ClearFix recommendation or guarantee. Services are performed by an independent specialist. Customers must independently assess the provider and the documents required for the work. These statements do not exclude duties or liability that applicable law does not allow the Operator to exclude.

4. How Information Is Stored *

The app stores account and service data in Firebase services configured for ClearFix. Firebase Authentication stores authentication records. Cloud Firestore stores profiles, legal consents, moderation state, orders, proposals, conversations, messages, notifications, complaints, settlements, commission-payment state, and related metadata. Firebase Storage stores profile and order photos and private settlement evidence. Firebase Cloud Functions process server-side workflows such as geocoding, order search, notifications, settlements, Stripe payment verification, moderation acknowledgement, and account deletion cleanup.

Sentry stores diagnostic error, release, session, route, and limited performance data sent by the app for monitoring and troubleshooting. The app-side controls described in Section 2 do not by themselves prove Sentry account-level IP scrubbing, region, or retention settings. Stripe stores and processes the payment and transaction data needed to host Checkout, prevent fraud, process the provider’s commission payment, and return payment status. Their retention practices are governed by their service terms and privacy controls, while ClearFix keeps only the records reasonably needed for its stated purposes.

OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure receives map-tile requests when a map is displayed. ClearFix does not send an account identifier with the tile URL, but the request can expose the technical data and approximate viewed map area described in Section 2. OpenStreetMap Foundation and its infrastructure providers handle that request under their own privacy and operational policies.

The app and browser may store local preferences such as language and theme in local app storage, browser storage, or SharedPreferences. Clearing local storage may reset these preferences but does not delete server-side account data.

7. Retention and Deletion *

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, accounting, payment reconciliation, moderation review, dispute, or backup reasons. Diagnostic events in Sentry and payment data in Stripe follow the configured service retention and deletion controls; ClearFix may retain a limited internal incident, payment, or audit record after the corresponding provider event is deleted when reasonably necessary for those purposes.

You can request account deletion in the app from Profile, Edit Profile, Delete account, or by using the account deletion information at https://www.clearfix.click/account_deletion_instruction.html. When your Firebase account is deleted, backend cleanup removes your user profile, user-scoped storage files, settings, and only the orders you created as a customer, including their ordinary order-scoped photos and records. Finalized settlements, commission payments, invoices, receipts, refunds, chargebacks, and supporting financial records are not deleted with the account or source order and instead follow the business and legal record schedule below. If you participated in an order created by another customer as a provider, proposal author, conversation participant, or complaint participant, the shared business history may be retained for the other participant, service integrity, dispute, accounting, security, and legal purposes. Retained shared records, including proposals, accepted terms, messages, closure comments, complaints, settlements, and public order-history references, are stripped of your display name, company name, profile photo, phone number, and other account-profile identifiers where they are no longer required, and your account is shown as deleted. Existing ordinary conversations with a deleted participant become read-only. Some backups, logs, legal records, or support records may remain for a limited period when required or reasonably necessary.

Account and service-history schedule: while your account remains open, we retain your account, profile, professional documents, legal-consent records, and order-related history, including proposals, messages, complaints, moderation records, and settlements. Keeping this history available is an ongoing ClearFix service purpose and supports repeat orders, account security, support, payment reconciliation, moderation, and disputes. ClearFix does not close or delete an account solely because it is inactive. You choose whether to request account deletion, subject to the limited retention described below.

Business and legal record schedule: commission payments, invoices, receipts, tax records, refunds, chargebacks, and the records reasonably needed to support those entries are retained for at least six years from the end of the last tax year to which they relate, or longer when required by law, an unresolved dispute, an audit, a legal hold, or a valid government requirement. A limited record of accepted legal-document versions, material moderation decisions, privacy requests, and related responses may be retained after account deletion when reasonably necessary to establish compliance, protect users, or resolve a claim. Records retained after account deletion are access-restricted and are not used to restore the deleted account or ordinary product history.

Technical and provider record schedule: Sentry diagnostic events are retained for up to 30 days. Ordinary application and service logs in Google Cloud's default log bucket are retained for 30 days; audit and system logs that Google places in its required log bucket are retained by Google for 400 days and that period cannot be shortened by ClearFix. If scheduled Firestore backups are enabled, they are retained for no more than 30 days. Support mailbox records are retained for up to 24 months after a request is closed, unless they form part of a payment, tax, unresolved dispute, security incident, legal hold, or other record subject to a longer period described in this policy. Temporary Stripe Checkout return-routing records used to deliver a payment result to the correct ClearFix client are scheduled for deletion 30 days after creation. Deleting those temporary routing records does not delete the commission payment record, receipt number, stored receipt URL, invoice, refund, chargeback, or settlement history. Under Stripe's current published rules, a hosted receipt does not expire, but its URL expires after 30 days; opening an expired URL prompts the payer to provide the email address associated with the original transaction so Stripe can send a new link. ClearFix-controlled payment and receipt records follow the business and legal record schedule above. Stripe retains the payment data and hosted receipts it controls under Stripe's own legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, and service-retention requirements.
