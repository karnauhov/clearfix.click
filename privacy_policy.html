1. Overview *

Last updated: November 25, 2026. Effective date: November 25, 2026.

This policy applies to personal information processing by ClearFix on and after the effective date, including the continued storage, use, or disclosure of information collected earlier. Processing completed before that date remains subject to the law and any policy applicable at that time. Your rights and our obligations, including the handling of requests about earlier information, are governed by applicable law. This policy does not itself authorize the use or disclosure of previously collected information for new purposes; any consent or other legal basis required by applicable law is still needed.

ClearFix is an app and website intended primarily for the Canadian service market. The website is available at https://www.clearfix.click/welcome. The service operator is CHYSTIAK OLEH, an individual (the "Operator", "we", or "us"). This policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and delete personal information. It covers accounts, profiles, orders, proposals, messages, files, maps, location features, address autofill, notifications, moderation, financial settlement, commission payments by providers, support, and related features.

We do not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or use personal information for marketing unrelated to the ClearFix service. Providers pay the ClearFix commission through Stripe Checkout. In this payment flow, Stripe collects payment-method details on a page it hosts, and ClearFix does not receive full card numbers or card security codes. We receive payment status and limited payment and receipt details as described in Section 2.

2. Information We Collect *

We collect and process the information described below, depending on the features you use, to run the service, protect accounts, complete order workflows, meet legal obligations, and support users. Additional information about specialist profiles and financial settlement is described in Section 3.

  • Account and authentication Email address, Firebase user ID, display name, profile photo URL, phone number, email verification state, whether the account is anonymous, and identifiers of sign-in providers. Firebase Authentication handles passwords and authentication tokens; ClearFix does not store your password in Firestore.
  • Profile and preferences Display name, phone number, preferred language, notification preferences, profile photo, and saved address details such as country, province or territory, city, postal code, street lines, unit or suite, buzzer, floor, PO Box, rural route, site, compartment, box, lot, concession, legal land description, and access notes when you choose to provide them.
  • Orders and service requests Order title, description, category, status, status history, photos, address and access details, location coordinates, where available, used for address suggestions and geographic order search, schedule, urgency, budget in CAD, whether you request a receipt for tax purposes, contact name, contact phone, preferred contact channel, customer notes, closure reasons and comments, and timestamps.
  • Specialist proposals and accepted terms Specialist and customer IDs, the specialist’s display or company name, profile photo and contact details needed for the proposal or order, proposed price, schedule, terms, warranty notes, proposal state, revision history, validity period, a saved copy of the selected provider’s details and accepted terms, and related timestamps.
  • Messages and conversations Conversation participants, order or proposal context, message text, message sender, participant role, message ID generated by the client app, sent time, read state, and last-message previews used to show conversation lists and unread badges.
  • Photos, files, and metadata Profile photos and order photos selected from your camera, gallery, or file picker, plus metadata such as original file name, content type, size, image dimensions, source, and upload time.
  • Location and address autofill If you grant device location permission, the app may request your current coordinates and send them through a Firebase Cloud Function to Google Geocoding so it can suggest a Canadian address. The app may also send address details you enter or save through a Firebase Cloud Function to Google Geocoding to obtain coordinates for location-based features; this does not require device location permission. When a map is displayed, the app requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap Foundation infrastructure, including when device location permission is denied. Those requests can include the requested tile coordinates, which indicate the approximate map area being viewed, and technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and referrer when supplied by the device or browser. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings.
  • Technical and support data Device platform, browser or operating system details, app and build version, locale, time zone, route and lifecycle context, error type, message and stack trace, session and limited performance context, and support messages you send to us. Firebase and other infrastructure providers may also process technical request metadata such as an IP address. We use this information to secure the service, diagnose failures, measure crash-free sessions, and respond to requests. ClearFix configures the Sentry SDK to disable its default collection of personally identifying information, screenshots, view hierarchy, SDK logs, and automatic user-interaction data, and removes the event user object before sending an event. These measures do not guarantee that error text, stack traces, or other diagnostic context contain no personal information. Sentry and its network infrastructure may still receive technical request data, including an IP address, subject to the project settings and Sentry controls described below.
  • Consent and moderation records Accepted legal-document versions and acceptance times; complaints; moderation warnings and the times you acknowledge reading them; reason codes and moderator messages; temporary-block start and end times; permanent-ban state; and audit or evidence records reasonably needed to apply, review, or defend a moderation decision.
  • Commission payment records Commission amount and currency, payment status, Checkout Session, payment, charge and event identifiers, payment-attempt status and the result of returning to ClearFix from Stripe Checkout, timestamps, receipt URL and number, payer name and email address, order reference and title, payment-method type, card brand and last four digits when supplied by Stripe, and reconciliation records. Stripe collects and processes the payment-method details entered on its Checkout page. ClearFix stores the limited payment and receipt details listed above in Firestore, but does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes.

3. Specialists, Providers, and Public Information *

The provisions about specialists apply to users who create a professional profile, search for orders, or submit proposals, even before they are selected as a provider for an order. We process selected service categories, search area and radius, professional headline, service description, experience, education, courses, certifications, service area, native and foreign languages, and voluntarily uploaded professional documents and their metadata. This information is used to find relevant orders, present specialists, and help customers select a provider.

An active signed-in user can access a specialist’s name, photo, account type, interface language, registration date, spoken languages, professional information and attached documents, as well as the counters and order history provided by the service. In this policy, public professional information means information available to other active signed-in ClearFix users; viewing the public profile requires sign-in. History may include an order’s title, outcome, dates, closure reasons and comments, complaint indicators, and information about an order’s unavailability. The service does not display the account’s email, phone number, or home address as separate public-profile fields. However, personal information that you include in professional text or attached documents can be seen by users who have access to those materials. Contact and site access details are disclosed to order participants according to their role and the order stage.

In connection with an order, we process proposals, agreed terms, the parties’ responses, work dates and outcomes, closure reasons and comments, messages, and complaints. Financial settlement involves declared payments and refunds, the cost of materials and tax amounts, invoices, receipts and other evidence, commission calculations, calculation revisions, and review records. The customer sees the payment amounts declared by both parties, whether those amounts match, and the settlement status. The full calculation, deductions, and supporting documents are accessible only to the appointed provider and specifically authorized support personnel; support access is logged.

While a complaint dialogue is active, any active signed-in user who can access the order can read it. Only the customer and appointed provider can post complaint messages; other viewers have read-only access. After the complaint is resolved, its messages are hidden from ordinary users but may be retained for moderation, disputes, security, and legal records, subject to the retention and deletion rules in Section 7. Ordinary private correspondence does not become public. A complaint or a displayed count does not, by itself, establish fault, dishonesty, or lack of qualification.

Posting, receiving, storing, technically processing, or displaying a profile, document, message, or complaint does not by itself mean that ClearFix has verified the information, confirmed a specialist’s qualifications or honesty, established a violation, or endorsed a user’s conduct. ClearFix does not currently verify every specialist or every professional document before display and does not continuously supervise services. A profile, order history, platform indicators, and absence of account restrictions are not a ClearFix recommendation or guarantee. Services are performed by an independent specialist. Customers must independently assess the provider and the documents required for the work. These statements do not exclude duties or liability that applicable law does not allow the Operator to exclude.

4. How Information Is Stored *

The app stores account and service data in Firebase services configured for ClearFix. Firebase Authentication stores authentication records and processes authentication data in the United States. Cloud Firestore stores profiles, records of legal-document acceptance, moderation state, orders, proposals, conversations, messages, notifications, complaints, settlements, commission-payment records, and related metadata. Firebase Storage stores profile and order photos, professional documents, and private settlement evidence. Firebase Cloud Functions process server-side workflows such as geocoding, order search, notifications, access to professional documents, settlements, Stripe payment verification, recording acknowledgements that moderation warnings have been read, and account deletion cleanup.

Sentry stores diagnostic information about errors, releases, sessions, routes, and limited performance data sent by the app for monitoring and troubleshooting. The app-side controls described in Section 2 limit what is sent, but do not by themselves determine Sentry’s server-side IP-address scrubbing, data-storage location, or retention settings.

Stripe stores and processes payment and transaction data needed to host Stripe Checkout, prevent fraud, process the appointed provider’s ClearFix commission payment, and return payment status to ClearFix. Stripe may also retain records to meet its own legal and regulatory obligations and prevent fraud.

OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure receives requests for map images (tiles) when a map is displayed. ClearFix does not include a ClearFix account identifier in the tile URL, but these requests may expose technical data and the approximate viewed map area as described in Section 2. OpenStreetMap Foundation and its infrastructure providers handle these requests under their own privacy and operational policies.

The app and browser may store local preferences such as language and theme in device or browser storage. Firebase components in the app may also retain sign-in state and cached copies of service data on the device, depending on the platform and settings. Clearing the relevant local storage may reset preferences, sign you out on the device, or remove cached copies, but does not delete your server-side account or records.

Retention and deletion are described in Section 7, and international processing is described in Section 11. Service-provider terms and settings do not replace the Operator’s obligations under applicable privacy law for personal information under its control. You can request written information about our policies and practices for service providers, including those outside Canada, or ask questions about storage by contacting the Privacy Officer identified in Section 12.

7. Retention and Deletion *

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and for any period required by applicable law. This includes limited retention for security, accounting, payment reconciliation, moderation review, disputes, and backups, subject to the schedules below. An open or inactive account does not by itself justify keeping information that is no longer needed. A limited internal incident, payment, or audit record may remain after a corresponding Sentry event or Stripe record is deleted, only where still reasonably necessary for those purposes or required by law.

Requesting deletion: you can request account deletion in the app from Profile, Edit Profile, Delete account, or follow the instructions at https://www.clearfix.click/account_deletion_instruction.html. The self-service process requires recent authentication and can be paused by active orders, including saved drafts and orders assigned to you, unresolved financial settlements or disputes, outstanding balances, or a seven-day hold after order closure. These technical conditions do not override your rights under applicable law. For help with a blocked request or to exercise your privacy rights, contact clearfix.ca@gmail.com; Section 9 explains those rights.

Account cleanup: deletion is processed on the server in stages, rather than instantly when you submit the request. The cleanup removes your user profile, user-scoped storage files, settings, and only the orders you created as a customer, including their ordinary order-scoped photos and records. Finalized settlements, commission payments, invoices, receipts, refunds, chargebacks, and supporting financial records are not deleted with the account or source order and instead follow the business and legal record schedule below. A limited reference to a deleted order can remain in another participant’s public order history, including its title, outcome, dates, closure reasons and comments, and an indication that the order is unavailable, subject to the shared-history limits below.

Shared history: if you participated in an order created by another customer as a provider, proposal author, conversation participant, or complaint participant, shared business history may be retained for the other participant, service integrity, disputes, accounting, security, and legal purposes, only to the extent and for as long as reasonably necessary or legally required. Cleanup removes your display name, company name, profile photo, phone number, and other account-profile details from the participant snapshots it processes, and marks the account as deleted. This does not erase personal information you entered in message text, comments, accepted terms, or supporting documents, or necessarily remove internal account identifiers needed to link retained records. Retained history is therefore not necessarily anonymous. Existing ordinary conversations with a deleted participant become read-only. Access restrictions continue to apply as described in Sections 3, 6, and 8.

Account and service-history schedule: while your account remains open, we retain the account, profile, professional documents, legal-consent records, and order-related history needed for the service, including proposals, messages, complaints, moderation records, and settlements. Keeping this history available supports repeat orders, account security, support, payment reconciliation, moderation, and disputes, subject to the necessity and legal limits above. ClearFix does not close or delete an account solely because it is inactive. You choose whether to request account deletion, subject to the limited retention described in this section.

Business and legal record schedule: commission payments, invoices, receipts, tax records, refunds, chargebacks, and the records reasonably needed to support those entries are retained for at least six years from the end of the last tax year to which they relate, or longer when required by law, an unresolved dispute, an audit, a duty to preserve records, or a valid government requirement. A limited record of accepted legal-document versions, material moderation decisions, privacy requests, and related responses may be retained after account deletion when reasonably necessary to establish compliance, protect users, or resolve a claim. Mandatory retention periods, including those protecting access to information used to make a decision about you, take precedence. Where PIPEDA applies, records of breaches of security safeguards are kept for at least 24 months after the day we determine that the breach occurred. Records retained after account deletion are access-restricted and are not used to restore the deleted account or ordinary product history.

Technical record schedule: Sentry diagnostic events are retained for up to 30 days under the current Developer plan. Ordinary application and service logs in Google Cloud's _Default log bucket are retained for 30 days; logs that Google places in its _Required log bucket are retained by Google for 400 days, and ClearFix cannot change that period. If scheduled Firestore backups are enabled, they are retained for no more than 30 days. Deleting an account does not mean that every corresponding backup or log entry is immediately erased; retained copies remain subject to their applicable retention periods and access restrictions.

Support record schedule: support mailbox records are retained for up to 24 months after a request is closed, unless they are needed for payment or tax records, an unresolved dispute, a security incident, compliance with a duty to preserve records, or another purpose subject to a longer period under the business and legal record schedule above. Such an exception applies only to the information and period reasonably necessary or required by law.

Stripe records: temporary Stripe Checkout return-routing records used to deliver a payment result to the correct ClearFix client are scheduled for deletion 30 days after creation. Cleanup runs periodically, so this is not a promise of deletion at the exact 30-day mark. Deleting those temporary routing records does not delete the commission payment record, receipt number, stored receipt URL, invoice, refund, chargeback, or settlement history. Under Stripe's current published rules, a hosted receipt does not expire, but its URL expires after 30 days; opening an expired URL prompts the payer to provide the email address associated with the original transaction so Stripe can send a new link. This does not promise that Stripe will retain every record indefinitely. ClearFix-controlled payment and receipt records follow the business and legal record schedule above. Stripe retains the payment data and hosted receipts it controls under Stripe's own legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, and service-retention requirements.

8. Security *

ClearFix uses access controls to help protect personal information. These include Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Storage access rules, server-side checks of sign-in status and access permissions, limits on the data available to each user according to their role, verification of Stripe webhook signatures, and restricted access for support personnel. Access to financial supporting documents, moderation information, and diagnostic data is limited according to the person's role and the purpose of access.

The app's Sentry configuration limits the personal information sent for diagnostics, as described in Sections 2 and 6. These controls do not guarantee that diagnostic data contains no personal information.

We maintain safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information and the risks involved in processing it. If a personal information security breach occurs, we assess the risks and keep a record of the incident. We notify affected individuals and report to the relevant privacy authorities in the circumstances and within the time limits required by law.

9. Your Choices and Rights *

You can update many details in your account profile and on order screens and change the app language. See Section 7 for how to request account deletion.

Your rights depend on the privacy law that applies to your request, such as the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) or an applicable provincial law. You can ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access to it, ask how we use or have used it and to whom we have disclosed it, or request a correction, subject to the limits of the applicable law. You can also ask questions or complain about how we handle personal information.

If Quebec’s private-sector privacy law applies, you can request a copy in a structured, commonly used technological format of personal information we collected from you and hold electronically. You can ask us to send it to you or to a person or organization legally authorized to collect it. This right excludes information created or inferred from personal information about you and is subject to the statutory exception for serious practical difficulties.

Under the same Quebec law, you can ask us to stop disseminating personal information about you or to remove from an index a link associated with your name that gives access to that information. In certain cases you can also ask us to put a link back in the index. Section 28.1 of that law sets the conditions for these requests.

You can withdraw consent for processing that relies on it, subject to any notice requirements and limits permitted by applicable law. We will explain the consequences of withdrawal, which may include losing access to features that need the information. Once the withdrawal takes effect, we will stop the processing it covers unless the law permits or requires us to continue. We may still keep certain records for the purposes and periods described in Section 7 where the law permits or requires it.

To make a privacy request or complaint, write to our Privacy Officer at clearfix.ca@gmail.com. Describe your request or concern and identify the account it relates to, if there is one. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and, if you are acting for someone else, your authority to do so.

We will investigate privacy complaints and respond within the time required by applicable law. We will explain any refusal, limitation, extension, or fee to the extent the law allows. Where required by law, we will keep information covered by an access or correction request for as long as necessary to let you exercise your review rights. We will give you any advance notice of fees and information about review options and deadlines that the law requires.

If our response does not resolve your concern, or if we do not respond within the applicable time limit, you can complain to or seek review from the competent privacy regulator. The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada’s guide to finding the right regulator can help you identify the federal or provincial office that handles your concern.
