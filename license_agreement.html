8. Provider Commission and Financial Terms *

The provider’s commission is 5% of the Commission base under the rate and rules version recorded by ClearFix on the server in the agreed order terms. All calculation amounts are stored as whole Canadian dollar cents (CAD). The same formula applies to successful completion, cancellation by the customer, and cancellation by the provider: Commission base = actual payment received - sales tax included in that payment - allowable materials deduction. Cancellation before a provider is appointed requires no financial settlement.

Actual payment is the total paid to the provider for the entire order, including deposits and separate transfers, less refunds already made to the customer. It excludes promised future payments and purchases the customer makes directly from a store. An unreturned deposit is not automatically compensation: settlement remains pending until the parties agree its refund or final retention as compensation for services or cancellation. Final agreed retention is included under the same formula. For hourly orders, actual total payment is used without requiring a time log for commission calculation.

The materials deduction is the documented cost to the provider of materials attributable to the order. Customer purchases, returned goods, personal expenses, reusable tools and purchase tax recoverable by the provider are excluded. The provider’s markup remains in the Commission base. The provider declares one total materials deduction and supplies receipts; item-by-item transcription is not required.

The tax deduction is the exact sales tax attributable to the stated payment and supported by the provider’s invoice. ClearFix does not determine the provider’s tax status, rates or tax obligations. Personal income taxes are not deductible. One document may support both tax and materials if it contains the necessary information. Technical file validation does not establish the truth or eligibility of a claimed expense.

The customer states the total paid and the provider states the total received; both confirm the same amount and whether all payments and refunds are settled. Materials and tax are the provider’s documented declaration; separate customer approval of each deduction is not required. ClearFix distinguishes awaiting response, payment pending, review required, and finalized states. Missing responses, unknown amounts, and confirmed zero are distinct. Silence is not consent, zero payment, or a commission debt. Work may end while payment or a refund remains unresolved, and the app may later request the missing response.

A discrepancy, missing or problematic evidence, repeated evidence, or deductions that reduce positive payment to zero or exceed it require review. An authorized support reviewer records their identity, reasons, and evidence. A substantiated loss may result in a zero Commission base, but no negative commission arises. Financial statements, calculations, and documents are available only to the customer to the limited extent shown in the app, the appointed provider, and explicitly authorized support with audited access.

When a completed or post-appointment cancelled order has a finalized positive Commission base, ClearFix calculates the commission on the server, rounds it to the nearest cent, and shows it to the appointed provider as payable. The provider pays the Operator through the protected commission page using the Operator’s Stripe Checkout account. Commission payments are received by OLEH CHYSTIAK. Stripe acts as the payment processor for this payment and is not the provider of the ClearFix platform service. ClearFix does not collect the customer’s payment to the provider and does not receive the provider’s full card number or card security code. A zero commission is marked as not requiring payment. A non-zero amount below Stripe’s minimum standalone CAD charge remains outstanding until ClearFix supports a lawful combined or alternative payment method.

The Operator is not registered for GST/HST as of the 'Last updated' date shown above and does not collect GST/HST on the ClearFix commission. This position depends on the Operator continuing to qualify as a small supplier under applicable law. The Operator monitors the registration threshold. If registration becomes mandatory or the Operator registers voluntarily, ClearFix will update the checkout disclosure, receipts or invoices, and this Agreement before beginning to collect applicable GST/HST. The Operator is the supplier and issuer of receipts or invoices for the ClearFix commission. Stripe processes the payment and hosts or emails the payment receipt on the Operator's behalf; ClearFix may also display a link to that receipt. The Operator will provide any additional invoice or tax information required by applicable law upon request at clearfix.ca@gmail.com.

A duplicate or technically erroneous commission payment will be refunded. If a confirmed settlement correction reduces the commission below the amount already paid, the Operator will refund the overpaid difference. Refunds are issued through Stripe to the original payment method, and the time when funds become available depends on Stripe, the payment network, and the payer's financial institution. A correctly calculated and processed commission is not refundable merely because the provider later changes their mind or stops using ClearFix. Filing a payment dispute or chargeback does not by itself automatically suspend or terminate platform access. The Operator may review the evidence, correct the settlement record, respond to an improper dispute, and restore any amount that remains confirmed and unpaid to the provider's outstanding balance, which is then subject to the ordinary access restrictions described in this Section. Nothing in this Agreement limits a refund, correction, chargeback, or other payment remedy that cannot lawfully be limited.

Returning from Stripe Checkout, closing the browser page, or seeing a client-side success message does not prove payment. ClearFix marks the commission paid only after a signed Stripe event or a server verification of the Checkout Session confirms the exact order, provider, currency, and amount. A cancelled, expired, incomplete, or failed Checkout attempt leaves the commission unpaid. While any provider commission remains unpaid, ClearFix may block new proposals and creation of new conversations while keeping existing conversations available. A later refund or additional payment creates a new settlement revision and adjustment; changing the payment requires the other party’s response, while changing deductions alone preserves confirmation of an unchanged payment. Repeating an order starts a new calculation without copying prior payments or receipts. The Commission base is a platform calculation, not the provider’s accounting net income.
