8. Provider Commission and Financial Terms *

The commission payable by the provider to the Operator is 5% of the Commission base under the rate and rules version recorded by ClearFix on the server in the agreed order terms. The minimum payment rules below also apply. All calculation amounts are stored as whole Canadian dollar cents (CAD). The same formula applies to successful completion, cancellation by the customer, and cancellation by the provider: Commission base = actual payment received - sales tax included in that payment - allowable materials deduction. ClearFix does not create a settlement record for an order cancelled before a provider is appointed. This does not extinguish any payment or refund obligation arising under applicable law or a separate agreement.

Actual payment is the total paid to the provider for the entire order, including deposits and separate transfers, less refunds already made to the customer. It excludes promised future payments and purchases the customer makes directly from a store. An unreturned deposit is not automatically compensation: settlement remains pending until the parties agree its refund or final retention as compensation for services or cancellation. Final agreed retention is included under the same formula. This calculation does not authorize retention contrary to the transaction terms or applicable law. For hourly orders, actual total payment is used without requiring a time log for commission calculation.

The materials deduction is the documented cost to the provider of materials attributable to the order. Customer purchases, returned goods, personal expenses, reusable tools and purchase tax recoverable by the provider are excluded. The provider’s markup remains in the Commission base. The provider declares one total materials deduction and supplies receipts; item-by-item transcription is not required.

For this commission calculation, the sales-tax deduction is the exact sales tax lawfully included in the stated payment and supported by the provider’s invoice or other legally required tax document, taking account of refunds and adjustments. ClearFix does not determine the provider’s tax status, rates or tax obligations. Taxes on the provider’s income are not deductible from the Commission base. One document may support both tax and materials if it contains the necessary information. Technical file validation does not establish the truth or eligibility of a claimed expense.

The customer states the total paid and the provider states the total received, in each case after deducting refunds already made to the customer. To finalize the settlement, both must confirm the same amount and that all payments and refunds are settled; otherwise, each must indicate what remains unresolved. The provider declares and documents the materials and sales-tax deductions; separate customer approval of each deduction is not required. ClearFix distinguishes awaiting response, payment pending, review required, and finalized states. Missing responses, unknown amounts, and confirmed zero are distinct. Silence does not by itself constitute consent, confirm zero payment, or create a commission debt. Work may end while payment or a refund remains unresolved, and the app may later request the missing response. Once both parties confirm the same amount and that payments and refunds are settled, ClearFix locks that amount. When review of any deductions is complete, ClearFix fixes the Commission base and the commission using that agreed amount.

A discrepancy, missing or problematic evidence, repeated evidence, or deductions that reduce positive payment to zero or exceed it require review. An authorized support reviewer records their identity, reasons, and evidence. Support cannot replace either party’s payment confirmation or finally determine a legal dispute between them; the parties retain their rights under the transaction and applicable law. A substantiated loss may result in a zero Commission base, but no negative commission arises. Within ClearFix, the customer can see only the parties’ stated payment amounts, whether they match, and the settlement status. The full calculation, deductions, and supporting documents are accessible only to the appointed provider and explicitly authorized support personnel whose access is logged. Processing by infrastructure providers and any other lawful disclosure remain subject to Section 16 and the Privacy Policy.

When a completed or post-appointment cancelled order has a finalized positive Commission base, ClearFix calculates the commission on the server, rounds it to the nearest cent, with half a cent rounded up, and shows it to the appointed provider as payable. The provider pays the Operator through the protected commission page using Stripe Checkout connected to the Operator’s Stripe account. Commission payments are received by CHYSTIAK OLEH. Stripe acts as the payment processor for this payment and is not the provider of the ClearFix platform service. ClearFix does not collect the customer’s payment to the provider and does not receive the provider’s full card number or card security code. A zero Commission base or a zero commission rate requires no payment. For a positive base and rate, the commission is at least CAD 0.50, matching Stripe’s published minimum standalone CAD charge. This minimum can make the commission exceed 5% of the Commission base. ClearFix shows the total commission, amount already paid, and balance due before opening Stripe Checkout.

The Operator is not registered for GST/HST as of the 'Last updated' date shown above and does not collect GST/HST on the ClearFix commission. This position depends on the Operator continuing to qualify as a small supplier under applicable law. The Operator monitors the registration threshold. If registration becomes mandatory or the Operator registers voluntarily, ClearFix will update the checkout disclosure, receipts or invoices, and this Agreement before beginning to collect applicable GST/HST. Statutory registration, collection, and remittance obligations apply from the date required by law and cannot be postponed by an update to this Agreement. This GST/HST statement does not determine any separate provincial sales-tax obligations. The Operator is the supplier and issuer of receipts or invoices for the ClearFix commission. Stripe processes the payment and hosts or emails the payment receipt on the Operator's behalf; ClearFix may also display a link to that receipt. The Operator will provide any additional invoice or tax information required by applicable law upon request at clearfix.ca@gmail.com.

The Operator will refund duplicate or technically erroneous commission payments. For questions about a commission charge or refund, contact clearfix.ca@gmail.com. The Operator issues refunds through Stripe to the original payment method; the time when funds become available depends on Stripe, the payment network, and the payer’s financial institution, subject to mandatory legal deadlines. If that refund fails, the Operator remains responsible for providing the refund through a lawful alternative. A correctly calculated and processed commission is not refundable merely because the provider later changes their mind or stops using ClearFix. Filing a payment dispute or chargeback does not by itself automatically suspend or terminate platform access. The Operator may review the evidence, respond to an improper dispute, and restore any amount that remains confirmed and unpaid to the provider's outstanding balance, which is then subject to the ordinary access restrictions described in this Section. Any reinstated balance must remain legally due and take account of refunds, chargebacks, and amounts already recovered so that the same amount is not collected twice. Nothing in this Agreement limits a refund, correction, chargeback, or other payment remedy that cannot lawfully be limited.

Returning from Stripe Checkout, closing the browser page, or seeing a client-side success message does not prove payment. ClearFix marks the commission paid only after a signed Stripe event or a server verification of the Checkout Session confirms successful payment for the exact order, provider, currency, and amount.
